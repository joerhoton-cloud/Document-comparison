Media Monitoring Services Agreement

This Media Monitoring Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of January 1, 2026 by and between Provider Inc. ("Provider") and Example Corp. ("Customer").

1. Services

Provider shall deliver daily media monitoring reports covering print, broadcast and online sources in the United States and Canada.

Reports will be delivered by email no later than 8:00 a.m. Eastern Time each business day.

2. Fees and Payment

Customer shall pay an annual subscription fee of $48,000, payable quarterly in advance. Invoices are due within thirty (30) days of receipt.

Late payments shall accrue interest at the rate of 1.5% per month.

3. Term and Termination

The initial term of this Agreement is twelve (12) months and shall automatically renew for successive one-year terms unless either party gives sixty (60) days written notice.

Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach that remains uncured thirty (30) days after written notice.

4. Confidentiality

Each party shall protect the other party's Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information, but no less than reasonable care.

5. Limitation of Liability

In no event shall either party's aggregate liability exceed the fees paid by Customer in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

6. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Illinois.
